## About Me

Technology leader and strategist with **25+ years of hands-on experience** (since 1998), with distinguished tenure at global technology leaders such as **Microsoft** and **IBM**. I specialize in translating technological innovation into real, measurable business value, operating at the intersection of corporate strategy, artificial intelligence, data, and software engineering.

As a **Systems Builder**, my focus is bringing structure and clarity to ambiguous or highly complex scenarios. I consistently apply the **Triple Lens of Technology Evaluation**: 
1. **Technical Feasibility** (resilient, scalable architecture)
2. **Economics** (FinOps, TCO, pricing, and sustainable margin)
3. **Risk & Adoption** (security, compliance, governance, and organizational change)

My career spans from managing mission-critical DB2 Mainframe environments and security auditing to early leadership in IBM Watson across Latin America (2014) and driving enterprise AI adoption at scale for over 1,000 engineers at Microsoft.
